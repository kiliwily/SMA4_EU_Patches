# SMA4_Improvements

## Improvements
- Press Select in the mode selection screen to play only with Luigi. The option Luigi & Mario is the same as Mario & Luigi but with Luigi as Player 1 and Mario as Player 2.
- Press R in the item inventory to remove an item from the inventory without using it. Works in the main game and e-world.
- Mario and Luigi no longer powwer dowm when using a mushroom from the item inventory when their current item is better.
- Added wrap around (a.k.a. press down on the lowest entry in a menu to get to the top of the menu and vice versa) in several menus in the game that didn't have it.
- The lock in world 1 opens when you get the flute in 1-Fortress.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixes a bug that causes the Player to not get back to the overworld and stuck in the level when eaten by the big fish and pressing start to open the pause menu in the same frame.
- Fixes a bug that causes Mario and Luigi to have a brighter palette after a star runs out.
- Fixes a bug that causes the music to play faster when a str runs out in a level that has no time limit (e.g. pipe levels).
- Fixes a bug that cause Mario to get replaced by Luigi in a level replay and the item inventory to glitch out when pressing L after watching the replay.
- Fixes a bug that causes 8-Fortress to always sho a white flag, even if it wasn't beaten before.
